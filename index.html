Quick recap: the foundations Usul al-Din and Furu' al-Din, the ground everything below stands on

Usul al-Din · Roots of Religion

  1. Tawhid: the Oneness of Allah
  2. 'Adl: Allah's Justice
  3. Nubuwwa: Prophethood, sealed by Muhammad ﷺ
  4. Imama: the twelve Imams chosen by Allah to lead after the Prophet
  5. Ma'ad: the Day of Resurrection

Furu' al-Din · Branches of Religion

  1. Salat: prayer
  2. Sawm: fasting
  3. Hajj: pilgrimage
  4. Zakat: alms
  5. Khums: one-fifth of yearly surplus
  6. Jihad: striving in Allah's way
  7. Amr bil-Ma'ruf: enjoining good
  8. Nahy 'anil-Munkar: forbidding evil
  9. Tawalli: loving Allah's friends
  10. Tabarri: dissociating from oppression and from those who fought the Ahl al-Bayt

Why the history matters: the fourth root, Imama, is a belief about history. Every story below is an answer to one question: after the Prophet, who was meant to guide the Ummah, and what happened to them?

The five daily prayers · 17 rak'at

Three times a day, five prayers. You may pray each prayer in its own preferred time, or combine Zuhr with Asr and Maghrib with Isha.

Fajr2 rak'at
From true dawn until sunrise
Zuhr + Asr4 + 4 rak'at
One shared time, from midday until sunset. Pray Zuhr first, then Asr, together or apart.
Maghrib + Isha3 + 4 rak'at
One shared time, from Maghrib (after the eastern redness has gone) until midnight. Pray Maghrib first, then Isha, together or apart.

Combining is proven: Ibn Abbas narrates in Sahih Muslim that the Prophet ﷺ combined Zuhr and Asr, and Maghrib and Isha, in Medina "without fear and without travel", so that his Ummah would not be put in hardship. Praying each at its own preferred time (fadila) has extra reward, but combining is completely valid.

                Our way: proof in the heart, adab on the tongue

                The truth of the Ahl al-Bayt stands on the Qur'an and on proofs that even other Muslims' books record. It has never needed insults. At Siffin, Imam Ali heard his soldiers cursing the Syrians and told them: I dislike that you be cursers; describe their deeds instead.

                Imam Ja'far al-Sadiq told his Shia: "Be an adornment for us, and do not be a disgrace to us." People should see the Ahl al-Bayt in your honesty, your prayer and your manners. Our maraji' forbid insulting what other Muslims hold sacred.
